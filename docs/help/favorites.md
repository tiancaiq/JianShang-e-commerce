# Save and revisit favorite listings

## Common questions

- How do I save a listing?
- Where are my liked listings?
- Does liking reserve an item?

## Who this applies to

Signed-in users viewing an individual or business listing.

## Prerequisites

You must sign in to change your liked state or open **Favorites**.

## Pages

- [Listing detail](/listings/{listingId})
- [Favorites](/account/liked)

## Save a listing

1. Open a listing.
2. Select the heart/like count button in **Listing engagement**.
3. Wait for **Saving...** to finish.
4. Open **Favorites** in the marketplace navigation, or open **Account** and select **Liked Listings**.
5. Select a saved listing card to return to its current public detail page.
6. To remove it, return to the listing and select the liked button again.

## Availability and safety limits

- Liking a listing does not reserve it, lock its price, notify a seller of an offer, or create a purchase.
- A saved listing can disappear if it is closed, paused, removed, sold, or otherwise no longer public.
- The displayed count is marketplace engagement, not a seller rating or review score.
