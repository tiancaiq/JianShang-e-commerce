# Buy from an individual seller

## Common questions

- How do I buy an item from a person?
- Is there a buy button for individual listings?
- Does MSB process or protect my payment?
- How do the buyer and seller mark a trade complete?

## Who this applies to

Buyers viewing a listing labeled **Individual seller**.

## Prerequisites

- You can browse without an account.
- You must sign in before starting or continuing a conversation.

## Pages

- [Individual marketplace](/marketplace)
- [Listing detail](/listings/{listingId})
- [Messages](/account/messages)

## Arrange an individual trade

1. Open **Marketplace** and select an individual listing.
2. Review the **Description**, **Condition Notes**, price, public location, and images.
3. Select **Message seller**. If you are signed out, select **Sign in to message seller** and complete sign-in first.
4. Use **Write a message** and **Send** to ask about the item and arrange the handoff, payment method, and delivery or meeting details.
5. Agree on all terms directly with the seller. There is no structured offer form, cart, checkout, or platform order for an individual listing.
6. After the handoff is complete, the seller can select **Mark as done** in the conversation and enter **Qty sold**.
7. The buyer should select **Review confirmation**, read the disclosure, and select **Confirm completed** only after the handoff is actually complete.

## Safety limitations

- Individual payment and delivery happen off-platform. MSB does not process, hold, verify, or protect that payment.
- Do not treat a listing, message, or completed-trade status as a payment guarantee.
- Meet in a public place and avoid sharing an exact home address in chat.
- The buyer must be the buyer already in that listing conversation. A seller cannot choose a different buyer during completion.
- After buyer confirmation, the listing closes, disappears from public search, and the conversation becomes read-only.

For message controls and completion states, see [Messages and trade completion](messages-and-trade-completion.md).
