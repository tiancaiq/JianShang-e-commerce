# Sell an item as an individual

## Common questions

- How do I sell something?
- Why must I activate a seller profile?
- How do I add photos and submit a listing?
- Can I edit or close a listing?

## Who this applies to

People selling their own items through the marketplace account experience. Business staff should use the business seller portal instead.

## Prerequisites

- Sign in to a marketplace account.
- Accept the individual-selling disclosure.
- Use only a public city and county/region; do not enter an exact personal address.

## Pages

- [Trades and listings](/account/listings)
- [Seller profile](/account/seller-profile)

## Activate an individual seller profile

1. Open **Trades**, **Sell an Item**, or `/account/listings`.
2. On **Become a Seller**, read the **Off-platform trade disclosure**.
3. Select **I understand and accept the individual-selling terms**.
4. Enter **City** and **County / region**.
5. Select **Activate seller profile**.

## Create and submit a listing

1. From **My Listings**, select **New listing** or **Create draft**.
2. Choose **Category** and **Condition**. Complete any category fields marked required.
3. Enter **Title**, **Description**, optional **Condition notes**, **Price**, the three-letter **Currency**, public **City**, public **County**, and **Quantity**.
4. Select **Price is negotiable** only when you want to show that label.
5. Under **Listing images**, choose up to 10 JPEG, PNG, or WebP files. Each image must be 10 MB or less.
6. Select **Save draft**. New images are uploaded and attached after the draft exists.
7. Confirm that at least one image shows uploaded/review status and no selected image is still pending.
8. Select **Submit for review**.
9. While the listing says **Pending review**, its details and images are locked.
10. If it says **Changes requested**, update the requested details or images and select **Submit for review** again.

## Edit, close, or reopen a listing

1. Open **My Listings** and select the listing.
2. For a draft, make changes and select **Update draft**.
3. For an active listing, save the changed details and submit the changed version for review.
4. Select **Close listing** to stop an eligible draft or active listing.
5. A closed listing can be edited and submitted for review again.

## Availability and safety limits

- Individual listings create off-platform trades, not business orders. There is no platform payment, checkout, shipment, return, or refund flow for them.
- Use messages for free-text negotiation. Structured offers are not available.
- Publication depends on review and image moderation. Saving a draft does not make it public.
- Category requirements can change; the form may ask you to review newer category fields before submission.
- In AI-enabled demo builds, an **Optional AI draft assistant** may suggest a title, description, or category from saved listing images. It changes nothing until you review exact fields and select **Confirm and update listing**; it never submits the listing.
- To close a completed trade and update the completed-sale count, use the seller/buyer confirmation flow described in [Messages and trade completion](messages-and-trade-completion.md).
