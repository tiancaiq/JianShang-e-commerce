# Browse and search the marketplace

## Common questions

- How do I find an item?
- How do I filter by condition, price, or location?
- Where do I find business-store items?
- Why are business items not mixed with individual listings?

## Who this applies to

Anyone can browse public listings without signing in. Signing in is required only for actions such as liking a listing, messaging an individual seller, reporting content, or adding an eligible business item to the cart.

## Prerequisites

No account is required for browsing.

## Pages

- [Individual marketplace](/marketplace)
- [Business catalog](/stores)
- [Listing detail](/listings/{listingId})
- [Public store profile](/stores/{storeSlug})

## Search individual listings

1. Open **Marketplace**.
2. Enter words in **Search marketplace** or in the large search field and select **Search**.
3. Choose a category from the category rail, or leave **All categories** selected.
4. Select **Filters**, then choose **Condition**, **Min price**, **Max price**, **City**, or **County**.
5. Select **Apply filters**.
6. Use **Sort by** for newest or price ordering, or use the shortcuts **All listings**, **New Arrivals**, and **Under $50**.
7. Open a result to see its description, condition, public location, images, seller type, price, and availability.
8. Select **Load more** when it is shown.

## Search business items and stores

1. Open **Stores**. Business catalog search is separate from the individual marketplace.
2. Enter a product, store, or category in the business search field and select **Search**.
3. Select **Filters** to choose **Category**, **Condition**, a price range, **City**, **County**, or **Sort by**.
4. Select **Apply**.
5. Select **View item** to open a business listing, or select the store name to open its public profile.
6. Use **Clear filters** to return to the full business catalog.

## Availability and safety limits

- **Marketplace** returns active public individual listings. **Stores** returns active public business listings. The two purchase paths are intentionally separate.
- Search results can change when a listing is paused, closed, removed, sold, or no longer public.
- Public locations are limited to city or region context. Exact personal addresses are not shown for individual listings.
- A **Verified business** label reflects the application's approved-business state; it is not a promise about a product, payment, delivery, refund, or buyer protection.
