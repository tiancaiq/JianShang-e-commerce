# Open and follow a support request

## Common questions

- How do I contact marketplace support?
- What information should I include?
- Where can I see support replies?
- Does choosing **Refund** issue a refund?

## Who this applies to

Signed-in buyers, individual sellers, business applicants, and business staff who need account, order, payment, refund, selling, business verification, listing, or technical help.

## Prerequisites

Sign in and gather only the relevant reference IDs visible to your account. Do not include passwords, tokens, card data, bank credentials, or unnecessary private information.

## Pages

- [Your support requests](/support)
- [New support request](/support/new)
- [Support request detail](/support/{ticketId})

## Create a support request

1. Open **Account**, select **Support**, then select **New support request**.
2. Under **What do you need help with?**, choose **Account**, **Order**, **Payment**, **Refund**, **Selling**, **Business verification**, **Listing**, **Technical issue**, or **Something else**.
3. Enter a **Subject** of at least 5 characters.
4. Under **What happened?**, enter at least 20 characters with the facts support needs.
5. Under **Relevant reference (optional)**, add an **Order ID**, **Business ID**, or **Listing ID** only when it belongs to your account or is visible to you.
6. Select **Submit support request**.

## Follow and reply to a request

1. Open **Your support requests**.
2. Select a request to see its status, messages, related references, and history.
3. When the request is not resolved, enter **Your reply** and select **Send reply**.
4. If resolved, review **Resolution** and the ticket history. Resolved requests do not show the reply form.

## Availability and safety limits

- A support category describes the help needed; it does not itself create an order action, payment, refund, return, appeal, or enforcement.
- Support responses and status changes appear on the ticket page. The current project does not promise email, SMS, push, or realtime delivery.
- Support validates linked references and account visibility. An arbitrary ID does not grant access to another user's information.
- For a safety allegation about a public listing or business, use [Reports and appeals](reports-and-appeals.md). For an enabled business-order problem, use the store-group dispute flow in [Demo orders, cancellations, returns, and disputes](demo-orders-cancellations-returns-and-disputes.md).
