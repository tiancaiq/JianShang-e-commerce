# Use messages and complete an individual trade

## Common questions

- How do I start a conversation?
- Where is my inbox?
- Can I message myself about my own listing?
- How does a seller mark a trade done?
- What happens after the buyer confirms?

## Who this applies to

Signed-in buyers and individual sellers. Business-store checkout and order communication is not handled through listing chat.

## Prerequisites

- The listing must be a public individual listing.
- The buyer and seller must use the conversation created for that listing.

## Pages

- [Start from a listing](/listings/{listingId})
- [Inbox](/account/messages)
- [Specific conversation](/account/messages/{conversationId})

## Start and continue a conversation

1. Open an individual listing and select **Message seller**.
2. In the listing chat drawer, enter text in **Write a message** and select **Send**.
3. To return later, open **Inbox** or the floating **Open marketplace messages** button.
4. Use **Search conversations** to filter the inbox.
5. Select a conversation, review the listing context and safety note, write a message, and select **Send**.
6. Opening a conversation marks it read.

## Complete the trade

1. After the item handoff, the seller opens the correct conversation.
2. Under **Trade completion**, the seller enters **Qty sold** and selects **Mark as done**.
3. The buyer sees **Confirm completion** and selects **Review confirmation**.
4. The buyer checks the item, quantity, seller, and off-platform payment disclosure.
5. The buyer selects **Confirm completed** only after the real-world handoff is complete.
6. The listing closes and is hidden from public search. The completed conversation becomes read-only.

## Availability and safety limits

- Only text messages are supported. There are no structured offers, payment requests, attachments, blocking controls, or realtime delivery guarantees in this flow.
- You cannot start a conversation with yourself about your own listing.
- Individual payment and delivery are arranged by the users. MSB does not verify or protect off-platform payment.
- A seller can mark done only for the buyer already bound to that conversation. Buyer confirmation happens exactly once.
- Chat shows public-safe identity and listing context; do not share exact private addresses or unnecessary personal information.
