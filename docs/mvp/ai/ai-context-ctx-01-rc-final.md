## Findings and disposition

1. **P1, unresolved acceptance:** explicit “second one” with only one active card was answered using the first card after Retry. Layer: Agent ordinal grounding/policy, with a one-card Product result as precondition. A small out-of-range guard and test were added. CTX introduction is not established; the behavior appears to be a pre-existing validation gap. Live browser retest is required.
2. **P1 for final gate:** cart/reference, Stop, and actor-isolation browser checks remain unverified; a downstream Auth Service read timeout prevented completion after the worktree Agent restart. Do not substitute historical API-only evidence for this gate.
3. **P2:** Angular transiently retained the retryable assistant alongside its replacement until refresh. Component fix/test pass, but not live-browser rechecked. Product search also supplied laptop accessories and a mouse pad for “wireless mouse,” and public business search could not surface the safe cart fixture. These are relevance/fixture issues, not evidence of a lost typed context constraint.
4. **P2 environment:** the isolated Agent's Auth identity request timed out and new browser sessions showed “Marketplace assistant is unavailable.” A temporary timeout increase did not cure it; proxy-bypass settings from the earlier successful process were not preserved on restart. Root cause was not confirmed. No shared Auth, gateway, or Product infrastructure was rebuilt to conceal the blocker.

No P0 or unauthorized commerce mutation was observed. No claim is made that an unverified scenario passed.
